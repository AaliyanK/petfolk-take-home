# Voice guide

Every written deliverable in this repo (README, how-i-worked, build.md, triage notes, the "why"
lines inside journeys) should read like Aaliyan wrote it, not like a model wrote it.

## Hard rules

- **No hyphens or em dashes as connectors.** No " - ", no "—". Use a comma, a period, parentheses, or split the sentence. Hyphens inside a real compound word are fine (fear-free, follow-up).
- First person. Direct. Say the thing.
- Mostly short sentences. The occasional longer one joined by commas is fine, that is how he writes.
- Plain words. No "leverage", no "robust", no "seamless", no "in today's landscape", no "it's not just X, it's Y".
- Lead with the point, then explain. Not the other way round.
- Concrete over abstract. Numbers, names, specific examples.
- Contractions are fine. A little informal is fine ("this is the whole game", "Meta is powerful but stupid").
- Tables where a table helps. The brief asks for this and he does it naturally.
- Do not hedge three times before making a claim. Make the call, note it, move on.
- Show the thinking. How the decision got made, what was considered, what got cut.

## Things that make writing sound like a model (avoid)

- Em dash asides mid sentence
- Rule of three everywhere ("clear, concise, and compelling")
- "Let's dive in", "It's worth noting", "That said,", "Moreover", "Furthermore"
- Every paragraph opening the same way
- Over signposting ("In this section we will...")
- Vague confidence ("This robust solution will seamlessly...")
- Ending on a tidy summary sentence that just restates the paragraph

## Reference: how he actually writes

> "It all starts with research. Doing deep research into my customer Avatars and ICP."

> "Meta is really powerful, but very stupid. It does the best it can do, with the creatives you give it, but we've seen it overspend time after time."

> "My philosophy is hire slow and fire fast. I setup KPIs that need to at least be held, and I do weekly or biweekly meetings to track all KPIs."

> "At the end of the day, if the campaign isnt working, it's usually a creative and targeting issue rather than a media buying issue. I always try to look for gaps."

Short. Practical. Opinionated. Gets to the point.
