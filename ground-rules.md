# Ground rules

Every one of the five journeys follows these. Question 2 is built on them. Most come straight from the brief. Where it is our call, it says so.

| # | Rule | Where it comes from | How it shows up in the work |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Design around the stack as it is.** Vetspire is the system of record. It emits events. Segment normalises and routes them. Braze sends email, SMS, push and in-app. Gladly is the humans, Giga is the AI in front of them. PetfolkCare is the membership. We do not redesign any of it. | Brief, "Our stack, as it exists today. Assume it and design around it." | Every trigger is a Vetspire event. Every send is a Braze channel. Every reply path is Giga then Gladly. |
| 2 | **Channels and costs are fixed, so spend follows need.** Cheap automated touches for everyone. The $4 agent call, the $6 nurse call, the $2.75 handwritten note happen only when a real signal fires. | Brief channel table, and the Biscuit example gating the nurse call behind "only if he replied 3". | Each journey has an unconditional cost and an if-everything-fires cost at the bottom. The human touches all carry an "only if" gate. |
| 3 | **Every message creates an inbound.** Reply-to lands in Gladly with a real person. A message that will generate "what does this mean" replies has to be answerable by Giga from the record, or it lands on humans, and the brief says the support team barely handles medical questions. | Brief, "Reply-to on anything we send has to land in Gladly with a real person reachable" and "Medical questions barely register." | Clinical questions are routed to the nurse queue on purpose. Education touches quote the vet so Giga can answer follow-ups from the same source. We do not send a message we cannot staff the reply to. |
| 4 | **Anything we send becomes the medical record.** A webhook copies every Braze send into the Vetspire chart. There is no medical review board. | Brief, "a webhook copies anything Braze sends into the pet's Vetspire chart" and "We have no medical review board sign-off on any outbound clinical content today." | We quote the vet, we do not generate new clinical claims, we keep clinical statements minimal, and we keep the volume down because every touch clutters a chart a vet has to read. |
| 5 | **No Vetspire API for this exercise.** We work from text exports. Any field a journey needs that the export does not carry is named, with what we would ask Vetspire for. | Brief, "You don't have Vetspire API access for this. If you need a field the export doesn't carry, say so and say what you'd ask for." | Each journey lists its data gaps. Running list across the five: structured vaccine next-due dates, message open and engagement data, contact-channel preference, membership status as a queryable field, weight history, BCS trend, appointment-booked and estimate-issued events. |
| 6 | **The transactional spine stays untouched.** Appointment reminders, confirmations, the post-visit note. The new layer is additive. | Biscuit example, day -7, "booking confirmation, existing spine, untouched." | No journey rewrites or removes an existing message. Every touch is something new, layered on top. |
| 7 | **Two human queues that never cross.** The tele-advice nurse handles anything clinical. The support agent handles cost, scheduling and payment. | Biscuit example, "She does not discuss the findings; anything clinical goes to the nurse queue." | In Quorra the titer question goes to the nurse and the $869 estimate goes to the agent. In Ikko the amputation questions go to the nurse and the payment plan goes to the agent. |
| 8 | **Tracks self-terminate.** Each condition track has an entry trigger and an exit trigger and stops on its own. | Biscuit example, "ears clear, otitis journey exits" on Condition Resolved. | Every track in every journey names its exit event. Weight exits on Weight Status Changed to normal. A resolved condition exits the track. |
| 9 | **Suppress while the vet is already on it.** A pet in an active vet-driven recheck loop does not get a marketing layer on top. Our call, from reading the records. | Our call. Immo2 is on a two-week recheck cadence, Ikko's nail was on a weekly one. A membership nudge landing in the middle of that reads as tone-deaf and clutters the chart. | Each journey has a suppression rule: while an open recheck or an active treatment course is on file, the recurring spine and any commercial touch pause. Clinical safety-net touches still fire. |
| 10 | **Consent is a real thing, not an afterthought.** The Biscuit example does not mention opt-in or opt-out. We do. | Our call. The webhook-into-chart plus no review board makes this non-optional. | Each journey assumes two separate permissions: a clinical-communications opt-in tied to the vet relationship, and a marketing opt-out that a STOP reply or a preference-centre setting honours. A pet with marketing opted out still gets the clinical spine. |
| 11 | **Key on the patient, not the client.** Client name is not a household key. The pseudonymiser reused a small name pool, so one client name maps to several unrelated pets, and multi-pet households are not linked in the data. | Our catch. "Guthrie Pellow" is on five unrelated pets across four states. | Journeys key on Patient ID and signalment. Where a household matters (flea cross-treatment, a second dog on the same med), we flag it as a field we would need from Vetspire rather than inferring it from a shared name. |

## Assumptions we are making

The brief says to decide and note it. These are the calls we made to get moving.

1. **The quote check normalises whitespace.** The source text wraps sentences across physical lines. We assume Petfolk's string match treats a run of whitespace, including a newline, as a single space, so a quoted phrase matches as long as the words are contiguous in the source. We still prefer a phrase that sits on one line when a good one exists, and the verification script checks both the raw form and the whitespace-normalised form.
2. **The transactional spine's exact wording is not in the records.** We describe those existing messages generically ("booking confirmation", "post-visit note") and never rewrite them.
3. **Segment and Braze carry events and attributes the text export does not.** The Biscuit example fires on `Appointment Booked` and `Estimate Issued` and reads `app installed`, `pfc_intake_status` and engagement recency. We assume the same are available to us, and we list the ones we lean on per journey.
4. **Channel costs are all-in per touch.** No volume pricing, no per-clinic variation.
5. **One journey covers one pet for twelve months.** We do not model the whole household in a single journey, though we flag where a housemate changes the content.
6. **The $1.50 per patient per year figure is a target to test, not a hard cap.** Each journey shows its own annual cost. The argument for tiering it lives in Q2.
7. **The journey's "now" is the record's most recent encounter**, or its `Date Generated` when there is no encounter. Relative day offsets run from there, the way the Biscuit example runs from day 0.
8. **A "declined" line means "not now", not "never".** Re-raising a declined service at a better moment, bundled where possible, is a core play, exactly as the Biscuit example re-raises the senior panel.
