The brief asks two things of Question 1. Q1a, write the full 12 month journey for five specific records, in the format of the Biscuit example. Q1b, go through all 25 records and give a one or two line call on each. This page is the map to Q1a. The five journeys are below. The 25 record triage is in question-1/triage.md.

Every journey follows the eleven constraints in ground-rules.md, read that first. The method that produced each one is in working/how-i-build-a-journey.md. Every judgment call, per pet, is in working/decisions-log.md.

In every journey, highlighted italic text is quoted word for word from that pet's record. Everything else describes what the message does or why. Petfolk verifies pet facts by string match, so a script re-checks every quoted span in the built HTML against the source file. Across the five journeys that is 193 quoted spans, zero misses. The script is working/scripts/verify_quotes.py, the span lists are in working/evidence/.

What every journey has in common

Each one is built as parallel tracks, not sequential steps, the way the Biscuit example is. A pet has several storylines running at once: a spine, a problem list, a household change, and each track has its own trigger and its own exit. Each touch names its channel and cost, the Vetspire or Segment event that fires it, and any conditional gate. The recurring rules, spend follows need, every message creates an inbound to a real person, everything sent becomes the medical record, the two human queues never cross, are in ground-rules.md.

Two things no journey does. It never fires a vaccine due message off the text export, because the export cannot reliably say which vaccine record is current, and a wrong "your pet is overdue" message becomes part of the chart. And it never makes a clinical claim an exam has not supported.
