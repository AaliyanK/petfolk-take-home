What the record does not carry, and what we would ask Vetspire for

This journey leans on events and fields the text export does not expose: Appointment Booked, Estimate Issued, Consult Note Added, prescription days-supply and refill-due, structured vaccine records with a current-record flag, weight as a time series, body condition as a coded field, an insurance-provider field, household links, and message engagement.

Left out on purpose

  • The amputation decision itself. That is the owner's call, led by the vet and the surgeon. The journey supports it and does not push it.
  • Household allergy management with Rico. The data does not link the two dogs, so we do not build on an inferred household.
  • The recessed vulva as its own track. One line in the discharge email is enough.

Inconsistencies caught in the record

  • One radiograph block reads LEFT MANUS and left front digit four, while the diagnosis, the problem list, the assessment and the discharge all say right front digit 4. Treated as a clinic template error, quoted the right-front phrasing.
  • Two bordetella records with different next-due dates, one in November 2026, one in March 2027. No vaccine-due message fires off the export. Vaccine reminders need a structured Vetspire feed that marks the current record.
  • The nail onset is Injury of nail - Jun 23, 2026 on the problem list and 07/03/2026 in a support note. Used the problem-list date.
