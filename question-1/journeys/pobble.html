What the record does not carry, and what we would ask Vetspire for

Current medication status with a real active or inactive flag, the fluoxetine question is the clearest case in the whole set. Appointment Booked, Completed and Cancelled events, so the journey can react to the 28 Sep blood draw and to a cancellation the way this owner has cancelled before. Estimate Issued for the dental. Lab Result events for the blood work, to gate the dental on a real clearance. Fear score as a coded field over time. Problem-list entries with a status, so atopy reads as monitoring. Weight as a series. Referral Made events. Household links, since Sindri is in the same home and the two dogs' behaviour problems are entangled.

Left out on purpose

  • Asserting a cardiac diagnosis. The finding is soft, was confounded by tremor and a drug reaction, and the heart was Normal rate and rhythm. No murmurs or arrhythmias auscultated, pulses strong and synchronous. on the calm exam. The journey raises the record's own question for the DVM and stops.
  • Pushing fluoxetine as marketing, or assuming its current status. The journey surfaces the question and routes to a nurse.
  • A full behavioural-training program. That is a vet or behaviourist referral. The journey supports it, it does not run it.
  • Working up the polydipsia flag directly. The 28 Sep panel will speak to it.

  • Breed is written three ways: Chihuahua on the Petfolk header, "Chihuahua - Mixed" on one outside record, "Rat Terrier" on another. Used the header.

  • "Toby" is used for Pobble in one intake's free text. No alternate or personal name is quoted. Keyed on Patient ID PT-306BA7.
  • The medication list shows fluoxetine active while the 30 Jul 2026 intake says no meds, just simparica trio. Treated as an open question, not resolved by the journey.
  • The murmur and arrhythmia were heard twice in Sep 2024 (Heart murmur and heart arrythmia!) under extreme tremor and an active drug reaction, and the heart was normal on the calm Jul 2026 exam.
