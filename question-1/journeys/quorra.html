What the record does not carry, and what we would ask for

Almost everything structured. A Petfolk vaccine history and due dates, a problem list, any exam with weight and body condition, PetfolkCare membership status, the outside clinic's bronchitis notes and chest radiographs and current medication, Estimate Issued, Appointment Booked and Completed events, and Lab Result events. This journey is largely a list of what Petfolk would need to pull in.

Left out on purpose

  • Any vaccine encouragement. The owner has declined, and consent is real. The journey gives information and stops. The only vaccine message is the factual anaesthesia-clearance and titer explainer in Phase B.
  • A stated cause for the bronchitis. That is a DVM judgement at the consult, not something a message asserts.
  • Assuming membership either way. It is not in the record, so the membership touch branches on the real status.

Inconsistencies caught in the record

  • The owner's forwarded email has apostrophes rendered as colons, for example "he:s never had bronchitis before". The journey quotes only fragments that avoid the mangled contraction.
  • Breed is written three ways: Yorkshire Terrier on the Petfolk header, "Mixed" on the outside lab, and "yorkie-poo" by the owner. Used the header, described him as a Yorkie cross.
  • The client name is reused across the corpus for different households. Keyed on Patient ID PT-6A2B6A.
  • The exam was quoted at $99, then corrected to and exam would be considered illness $135 in the same note. Used $135.
